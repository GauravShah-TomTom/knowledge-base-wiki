# Jose Martinez Teira

Engineer with expertise in the [[HD Basemap]] lane modelling pipeline, specifically the [[Leg Boosting|leg boosting]] production pipeline that corrects missing road legs at junctions.

Active in the `#adas-hd-basemap-lanes-general` channel. In May 2026 he responded to [[Gaurav Shah]]'s question about oversized junctions, explaining that missing legs at junctions (causing large junction areas) are fixed by the leg boosting pipeline, but raw automation output may still exhibit the issue. He noted that leg-boosted regions are not yet being delivered at scale. He confirmed that the junction at 54.2898788, 10.4342199 was correctly modelled in the most recent [[Orbis]] layer basemap.
