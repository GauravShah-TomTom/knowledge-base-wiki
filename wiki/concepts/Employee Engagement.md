# Employee Engagement

A measure of how committed, motivated, and satisfied employees are with their work and organisation. Typically captured through periodic surveys that produce:

- **Overall engagement score** — a composite index (commonly 0–100)
- **Favorability breakdown** — percentage of respondents who are Favorable, Neutral, or Unfavorable on each topic
- **Category scores** — scores per theme (e.g. Manager, Team Collaboration, Clarity, Action Taking)

## At TomTom
