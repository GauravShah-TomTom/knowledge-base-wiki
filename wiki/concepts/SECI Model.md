# SECI Model

Knowledge conversion framework developed by Ikujiro Nonaka (Hitotsubashi University) describing how tacit and explicit knowledge interact in a spiral of organizational learning.

**S** — Socialisation (tacit → tacit): sharing experience directly  
**E** — Externalisation (tacit → explicit): articulating tacit knowledge into concepts  
**C** — Combination (explicit → explicit): combining explicit knowledge from different sources  
**I** — Internalisation (explicit → tacit): learning by doing, embodying explicit knowledge
