# HITL (Human-in-the-Loop)

A design pattern for AI-assisted automation where a human reviews, approves, or corrects AI output before it becomes final — balancing automation efficiency with human oversight.

## Usage in SDLC context

Applied in the [[AI First SDLC]] approach at stages where AI output needs validation before acting on it:
- Epic → Backlog story creation: agent generates a first-pass backlog, human reviews before finalizing
- Sprint grooming: agent refines stories based on discussions, human confirms
- Sprint planning: agent creates task breakdowns, human verifies acceptance criteria
