# Rule 50311 — Incorrect Direction of Traffic Flow for Lane (Connected Lanes)

**Severity**: Error, Critical

## Summary

Rule 50311 fires when the direction of traffic flow on connected lanes is incorrect: the "from" lane should have traffic flowing towards the junction, while the "to" lane should have traffic flow away from the junction.

- **Count in DEU-01 FMO run**: 2,497 occurrences.
- **Example transaction**: `4f94fc28-8f6f-40a3-9c36-fa79ba469be6` at 53.9280544, 10.3162323.

## Root Cause

Incorrect lane connectivity generated by the derivation process. In the example transaction, two roads connect from both sides — via a junction and via another road — which caused connectivity to be created via the junction instead of via the road, leading to incorrect traffic flow direction on the lanes.

## Proposed Solution

Improve derivation to ensure lane connectivities are correctly populated with correct traffic flow direction.
