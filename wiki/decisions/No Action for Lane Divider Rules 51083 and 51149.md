# No Action for Lane Divider Rules 51083 and 51149

## Decision

No corrective action is to be taken for rules [[problems/Rule 51083 Incorrect Lane Divider Type|51083]] and [[problems/Rule 51149 Incorrect Lane Divider Type|51149]]. Lane dividers should be populated as per [[systems/HD Basemap|HD]] data / reality, even when the output triggers these violations.

## Rationale

Turn lane dividers being solid (which triggers rule 51083) can reflect actual road reality. The violations may occasionally be false positives when the HD data is correct. Any automated "fix" would risk overriding valid data.

## Context

Identified during the Lanes FMO violation review of zone DEU-01 (rules had 557 and 387 occurrences respectively).
