# Viva Glint

Microsoft's employee engagement and feedback platform, used by TomTom to run organisation-wide engagement surveys.

## Key facts

- Vendor: Microsoft (part of the Viva suite)
- Used for: Employee engagement surveys, sentiment analysis, favorability scoring
- Integration: Produces scored dashboards with favorability breakdowns (Favorable / Neutral / Unfavorable) and benchmarked category scores

## TomTom usage

TomTom uses Viva Glint to run periodic [[Employee Engagement Survey]]s. Results are presented as an overall engagement score alongside category favorability charts that identify organisational strengths and areas of improvement.
