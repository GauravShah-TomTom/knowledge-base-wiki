# Iris

Iris is the task management system used by map editors to review and validate lane transactions produced by the [[Lanes]] pipeline.

## Workflow

1. [[Transaction Manager]] sends transactions (created from [[Lanes Automator]] JSON output) to Iris as tasks.
2. Editors filter and work through tasks in Iris, verifying correctness against reality.
3. For QA of specific rule fixes, editors use priority filters to focus on transactions flagged by the pipeline.

## Priority field

When a transaction was generated by a [[DTFR]] default rule, the pipeline sets a high priority (e.g., priority 1) on the Iris task, while other transactions receive a lower priority (2–3). Editors can filter by priority to verify only the rule-specific transactions first, then sample from the rest.
