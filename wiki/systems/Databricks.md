# Databricks

Databricks is the cloud data platform used by the [[Lanes]] pipeline to run [[Data Preparator]] and [[Cross Link]] at scale. It processes data across [[H3 Tiles|H3 tiles]] in parallel. The Databricks job is called from [[Airflow]] as the first step in the end-to-end pipeline.
