# MIT — Map Issue Tracking

MIT (Map Issue Tracking) is the reporting system that receives output from [[MEDS]] detectors. It aggregates detected map errors and surfaces them for review and action, including routing them through the [[Auto-Graduation]] pipeline to [[TomTom]] for map fixes.
