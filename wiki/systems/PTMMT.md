# PTMMT

PTMMT is a system in the [[Route Divergence]] pipeline within [[MEDS]]. It receives map-matched driver traces from MapMatching and produces a sequence of road segments (segment sequence) that represents the path actually taken by the driver. This segment sequence is then compared against the Navigation Route Suggestions segment sequence by the Divergence Calculator to identify route divergences.
