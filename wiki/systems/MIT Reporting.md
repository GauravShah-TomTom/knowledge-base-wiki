# MIT Reporting

*Source: raw/scans/MEDS Overview.pdf*

MIT Reporting is the internal reporting/tracking destination for map error detections produced by [[MEDS]] detectors. All detector outputs (GeoCatch, TurnRestriction, OneWay, etc.) flow into MIT Reporting.

The acronym MIT is not expanded in the source material — likely "Map Issue Tracking" or similar internal nomenclature.
