# Map Content Portal

TomTom's internal portal for publishing and browsing map data releases. URL: `https://mapcontent.tomtom.com/`.

Releases are organised under `/catalog/releases/<release-id>`. For example, [[HD Basemap]] version `26070.001` was published at catalog release `167528030`.
