# AI First SDLC (Project)

Initiative to systematically integrate AI tooling across all phases of the software development lifecycle — from strategy through delivery — within the TomTom map data team.

## Goal

Augment or automate high-effort, low-differentiation SDLC activities (Jira hygiene, sprint ceremonies, backlog creation, PR review, debugging) so engineers can focus on higher-value work.
